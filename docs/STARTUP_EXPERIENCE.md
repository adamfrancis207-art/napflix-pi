# Napflix Startup Experience

## Goal

The device should feel intentional and cinematic from the first second of power-on.

Avoid:
- Linux desktop appearance
- terminal flashes
- browser chrome
- visible boot clutter
- cheap startup behavior

---

## Desired Sequence

1. Device powers on
2. Soft ambient startup sound
3. Napflix logo appears
4. Dark fade-in animation
5. UI loads smoothly
6. Ambient motion begins
7. Main navigation becomes active

Total target:
5–12 seconds perceived startup.

---

## Audio

Startup sound should feel:
- warm
- nostalgic
- subtle
- cinematic

Possible inspiration:
- HBO static era
- THX softness
- PlayStation 2 ambience
- old cable box startup sounds
- Winamp startup energy

Avoid:
- loud gaming sounds
- meme noises
- harsh UI bleeps

---

## Visual Style

Startup visuals may include:
- CRT glow
- VHS noise
- ambient particles
- faded film grain
- retro typography
- subtle scanlines

Avoid:
- RGB gamer visuals
- over-animation
- clutter
- fake futuristic HUDs
